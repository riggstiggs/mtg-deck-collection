# Deck Guide: Tom Bombadil (Once Upon a Time)

## Commander Strategy
**Tom Bombadil**
*   **Archetype:** 5-Color (WUBRG) Sagas / Storybook Value
*   **Bracket:** 3
*   **Core Goal:** Tom is the Storyteller. He's telling a collection of fairy tales that appear separate at first, then gradually reveal they all take place in the same Woods. Sagas are the stories; lore counters are the telling. When a Saga's final chapter resolves, Tom reveals cards off the top until he finds another Saga and puts it onto the battlefield free — one story ending as another begins. The deck wins on combat damage (a wide board or a single grown threat), not combo, and it has to function whether or not Tom is actually on the battlefield.

### The Same Woods
Beyond the Sagas and named fairy-tale cameos, a layer of cards (Korvold, Oko, Rankle, Voja, Sun Titan, Faeburrow Elder, Chulane, Yenna, plus the cottage/castle/path lands) don't belong to one specific tale — they're the shared geography and cast that make every story feel like it's happening in the same forest.

### Finding Neverland Nods
Not every fairy tale gets a full cast. Some get Scenes (multiple cards clearly telling the story), some get Cameos (one strong card), and some are just Nods — a single object or effect (Three Bowls of Porridge for Goldilocks, Land Tax for Rumpelstiltskin's bargain) that only works if it's also pulling real mechanical weight. See the Story Map below.

## 🛠️ Card Explanations

### 📖 Sagas — The Stories (11 Cards)
*   **The Huntsman's Redemption:** Little Red Riding Hood's huntsman — spares his mark, then rallies the forest to protect her. Beast token into a creature/land tutor into a double pump-trample finisher.
*   **The Princess Takes Flight:** Rapunzel-shaped captivity and escape — exiles a creature, grants flying, then returns what was exiled. Real removal stapled to a story beat.
*   **The Witch's Vanity:** Hansel & Gretel's witch — kills something small, makes Food, then curses one of your own creatures with a Wicked Role for value.
*   **Three Blind Mice:** The nursery rhyme verbatim — a Mouse token, copied twice, then a team pump. Four chapters, so it's a turn slower to trigger Tom than most Sagas here.
*   **Korvold and the Noble Thief:** A heist from a dragon's hoard — Treasure into Treasure into stealing straight from an opponent's library.
*   **The Apprentice's Folly:** The Sorcerer's Apprentice, structurally exact — copies a creature as temporary Reflections, then makes you sacrifice all of them. The drawback IS the folly. Best thematic fit in the whole deck.
*   **Welcome to Sweettooth:** The candy village that turns out to have teeth — Human token, Food, then counters scaled by how much Food you've made.
*   **Tale of Tinúviel:** Beren and Lúthien (Tinúviel) — a mortal and an elf-maiden's doomed, defiant romance. Indestructible, then reanimate, then lifelink.
*   **Gadwick's First Duel:** A direct curse scene — Cursed Role shrinks something to a 1/1, then scry, then copy a cheap instant/sorcery.
*   **The Mountain-king's Return:** A king returning from the mountain — loot/token, reanimate something cheap, then a counter. Tolkien-sourced, fitting for Tom.
*   **Battle of Frost and Fire:** Catastrophe sweeping the Woods that spares the Giants — 4 damage to each non-Giant creature/planeswalker, scry 3, then a card. Doubles as the deck's Saga-flavored board wipe.

### 🐺 Little Red Riding Hood — Full Scene (4 Cards)
*   **Ruby, Daring Tracker:** The girl on the path. Ramp that fits the story perfectly.
*   **Wicked Wolf:** The wolf — fight-based removal on a real body.
*   **Fierce Witchstalker:** Another wolf on the path, reinforcing the scene.
*   **The Huntsman's Redemption:** (see Sagas above)

### 🍬 Hansel and Gretel — Full Scene (8 Cards)
*   **Sweettooth Witch:** The witch herself.
*   **Greta, Sweettooth Scourge:** The witch-hunting girl, named for the tale.
*   **Witch's Oven:** The oven — sacrifice outlet feeding Korvold and the Food package.
*   **Golden Egg:** A magical object dropped along the path; cracks for Food and a color of mana.
*   **Trail of Crumbs:** The breadcrumb trail — card selection off Food.
*   **Welcome to Sweettooth:** (see Sagas above)
*   **The Witch's Vanity:** (see Sagas above)
*   **Gingerbrute:** A gingerbread golem that runs — cheap, hasty, evasive, sacrifices for life.

### 🌱 Jack and the Beanstalk — Full Scene from One Card (1 Card)
*   **Beanstalk Giant // Fertile Footsteps:** Cast Fertile Footsteps early to search a basic land to the battlefield (real ramp+fixing, not just an extra land drop), then cast the actual Giant later — power/toughness equal to your land count. Ramp, Adventure, and payoff Giant all telling the same story in one card.

### 🎭 Cameos (7 Cards)
*   **Stroke of Midnight:** Cinderella — destroy a nonland permanent, its controller gets a 1/1 Human. Real Wilds of Eldraine card with genuine "Ash's distant footsteps" flavor text.
*   **Charmed Sleep:** Sleeping Beauty — an enchanted, unwakeable curse holding a creature down.
*   **Glass Casket:** The other half of Sleeping Beauty — confirmed "Wicked Slumber" flavor text, cheap exile-removal for a small threat.
*   **Tale of Tinúviel:** (see Sagas above) — Beauty and the Beast-adjacent doomed romance.
*   **Eriette of the Charmed Apple:** Beauty and the Beast's cursed apple, offered freely.
*   **Syr Armont, the Redeemer:** The real GW "loving the Beast makes him stronger" payoff — grants Monster Role, pumps enchanted creatures.
*   **Gruff Triplets:** Three Billy Goats Gruff — three identical siblings crossing into combat together.
*   **Cursed Courtier:** A self-cursing lifelink Noble — the strongest available transformation/curse cameo.

### 🥣 Nods (2 Cards)
*   **Three Bowls of Porridge:** Goldilocks, told entirely through three bowls sitting in a cottage. Modal Food artifact — damage, tap, or lifegain, choose one you haven't already chosen. No Bears required.
*   **Land Tax:** Rumpelstiltskin's bargain that favors the patient — real Eldraine flavor tie ("The Goose Mother and Other Tales"). Catch-up land ramp when you're behind.

### 🌲 Same Woods — The Shared Cast & Geography (10 Cards)
*   **Korvold, Fae-Cursed King:** Explicitly *fae-cursed* — a dragon-noble hoarding what's sacrificed to him.
*   **Oko, Thief of Crowns:** Eldraine's own signature trickster-fae villain. Turns anything into a 3/3 Elk.
*   **Rankle, Master of Pranks:** A wandering trickster fae causing mischief wherever he lands.
*   **Voja, Jaws of the Conclave:** A wolf-king ruling deeper in the Woods than the one on Little Red's path.
*   **Sun Titan:** A literal Giant who returns fallen chapters to the page — Saga/permanent recursion.
*   **Faeburrow Elder:** The forest itself, personified — scales with the colors you're already playing.
*   **Chulane, Teller of Tales:** Turns every creature's entrance into the next beat of the story.
*   **Yenna, Redtooth Regent:** Literally retells a Saga by copying it — the "Tom has told this story so many times" thesis in card form.
*   **Realm-Cloaked Giant // Cast Off:** A catastrophe sweeping the Woods that spares your own Giants, then the actual Giant shows up after.
*   **Edgewall Innkeeper:** The innkeeper who's seen every traveler pass through — Adventure-synergy value.

### 🏰 Storybook Locations (Lands)
*   **Witch's Cottage, Gingerbread Cabin, Idyllic Grange:** Real places in the Woods — check-lands with genuine basic-type synergy.
*   **Restless Cottage, Restless Bivouac, Restless Vinestalk:** Locations that can wake up and become creatures late.

### ⚙️ Same Woods Utility — Interaction, Ramp, Protection (functional, not story-specific)
*   **Once Upon a Time:** The opening line of the whole deck — free if it's your first spell.
*   **Beseech the Mirror:** A literal magic mirror — Bargain-cost tutor for any card, including a Saga (the only tutor in the deck that can actually find one).
*   **Three Visits, Nature's Lore:** Forest-based ramp that fetches a land with a basic type — doubles as fixing thanks to the manabase's typed lands.
*   **Kenrith's Transformation, Chaos Warp, Epic Downfall, The End, Bake into a Pie, Feed the Cauldron, Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Krosan Grip:** Curses, banishments, and consequences — removal dressed as fairy-tale-appropriate fates.
*   **Toxic Deluge, Farewell:** Catastrophe sweeping the whole Woods at once.
*   **Heroic Intervention, Swiftfoot Boots, Greater Auramancy:** Fairy-magic protection — Greater Auramancy specifically shields the Sagas/Auras the whole plan depends on.
*   **Eternal Witness:** Retelling a spent story from the graveyard.
*   **The Great Henge, Golden Egg, Arcane Signet, Chromatic Lantern, Gilded Goose:** Ramp and card advantage engines.
*   **Happily Ever After:** The literal last page of the book — gain 5/draw 1 on ETB, with a 5-color/6-card-type/life-total alt-win condition that's incidental upside, never the plan.

## 🚀 Future Roadmap: Upgrades & Considerations

### 💡 High-Impact Tech to Consider
*Seriously researched, not currently in the 99 — candidates for the next pass.*

*   **Sorcerer's Broom:** A second Sorcerer's-Apprentice beat; cut from V2 only for slot economy.
*   **Faerie Guidemother // Gift of the Fae:** The real Fairy Godmother cameo; currently absent story.
*   **Curious Pair // Treats to Share:** Literal gingerbread-house flavor text; redundant with the already-full Hansel/Gretel scene.
*   **Totentanz, Swarm Piper:** The real Pied Piper cameo — cut because its payoff wants more Rats than this deck should commit to for one copy.
*   **Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki:** Excellent trickster/mirror Saga, but its chapter III transforms rather than sacrifices, permanently taking it out of Sun Titan/Yenna recursion range.

## 📜 Deck Changelog
*   **2026-08-15 — Draft 1:** Initial 100-card build from the original "Once Upon a Time" brief. Commander Tom Bombadil, 13 Sagas (incl. Urza's Saga as a land), 37-land manabase.
*   **2026-08-15/16 — Draft 1 Audit:** Three rounds of research (WOE ten-archetype audit, Throne of Eldraine legacy pool, Enchanting Tales/WOT bonus sheet, Card Kingdom archetype mapping). Flagged Urza's Saga, Fall of Gil-galad, History of Benalia, and The Mending of Dominaria as thematic stretches (DOESN'T BELONG). Found Once Upon a Time and Eladamri's Call cannot tutor Sagas — a real gap against Tom's own thesis. Corrected a rules misread: Urza's Saga does sacrifice itself after chapter III like any other Saga.
*   **2026-08-16 — V2:**
    *   **In:** Gadwick's First Duel, The Mountain-king's Return, Battle of Frost and Fire, Stroke of Midnight, Syr Armont the Redeemer, Beanstalk Giant // Fertile Footsteps, Beseech the Mirror, Cursed Courtier, Land Tax, Greater Auramancy, Happily Ever After, Three Visits, Three Bowls of Porridge, Glass Casket, Gingerbrute, Nature's Lore, 5 Triomes, 9 typed snow duals
    *   **Out:** Urza's Saga, Fall of Gil-galad, History of Benalia, The Mending of Dominaria, Song of Freyalise, Charming Prince, Grumgully the Generous, Beanstalk Wurm // Plant Beans, Eladamri's Call, Storyteller Pixie, Talion the Kindly Lord, Fierce Guardianship, Teferi's Protection, Regrowth, 5 Castles, Path of Ancestry, Reflecting Pool, Exotic Orchard, 2 Restless lands, 2 fetches
    *   **Reason:** Full rebuild off the Draft 1 audit — cleaned up the four thematically-unjustified Sagas, fixed the "no tutor can find a Saga" gap with Beseech the Mirror, and rebuilt the manabase around typed dual-land cycles (Triomes + snow duals) since ~43% of Draft 1's lands were tapped/functionally tapped in the exact early-game window that mattered most.
